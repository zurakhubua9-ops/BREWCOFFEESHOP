A FEW LITTLE DETAILS

Good
to know.

How do I choose a drink size?

Choose S, M, or L below your drink, or open its photo to compare volumes and prices. Espresso has Single and Double options.

Is the displayed price the full price?

Yes. The price updates to match your selected size. A latte is 5 ₾ for Small, 7 ₾ for Medium, and 8 ₾ for Large. These are sample menu prices.

Can I order online?

Not yet. You can explore the menu and choose sizes, but this website does not submit orders or process payments.

Where can I find allergen information?

Ask the café team before ordering if you have an allergy or dietary requirement. Verified ingredients and cross-contact information have not been added to this concept menu.
