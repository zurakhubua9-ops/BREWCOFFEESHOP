know.

How do I choose my size?

Choose S, M or L below your drink, or open its photo to compare volumes and prices. Espresso has Single and Double options.

Is that the full price?

Yes. Each price is the full price for your selected size. A latte is 5 ₾ for Small, 7 ₾ for Medium and 8 ₾ for Large. These are illustrative prices for this concept menu.

Can I order online?

This is a brand concept and interactive menu. Orders and payments are not available.

What about ingredients and allergens?

Verified ingredients and cross-contact information have not been added to this concept menu. For any real food purchase, confirm dietary requirements with the café team before ordering.
